# Personal Folio reference design audit

Chromium inspected the rendered Personal Folio homepage at 1920 × 1080, 1440 × 900, 1280 × 800, 1024 × 768, 768 × 1024, 430 × 932, 390 × 844 and 375 × 812. The audit used full-page captures, visible DOM rectangles, computed CSS, successful font requests, and screenshots while scrolling, opening the menu, hovering on a project and operating the FAQ. [Live reference](https://personalfolio.framer.website/)

## Responsive geometry

- The wide variant uses a 900 px hero, a centered navigation pill measuring 334 × 44 px at y = 12 and a 544 px hero copy column. Hero content starts at y = 160. The logo viewport measures 1028 × 90 px at y = 733. Each hero CTA measures 187 × 48 px with a 16 px gap and 12 px corners.
- At 1024 px, the hero content starts at y = 129 and the logo viewport starts at y = 605. Four 724 × 362 px work cards use about 30 px vertical gaps. The work headline retains the wide typography.
- At 768 px and below, the mobile variant uses a 328 × 54 px navigation pill at y = 8, a 328 px headline box at y = 282, stacked 312 × 48 px CTAs, and a 61 px logo viewport at y = 670. The first work heading group begins at y = 815. The reference exposes this mobile layout even at 768 px. The reference’s media queries change variants at 810 px and 1280 px. Resizing across both boundaries confirmed the change in the rendered DOM.
- At 1440 px the four 1024 × 500 px work cards start at x = 208 and y = 1187, 1767, 2347 and 2927. Desktop corner radius is 32 px. At 390 px the four 328 × 600 px cards start at x = 31 and y = 980, 1628, 2276 and 2924. Mobile corners measure 16 px. Art is right aligned on wide screens and below the CTA at narrow widths.
- Wide editorial cards measure approximately 351 × 383 px, with 32 px gaps. Mobile cards measure 328 × 383 px and stack with 28 px gaps. The reference shows two full editorial cards at 1024 px. The implementation keeps the third topic accessible through a compact third row.
- The services cards are 374 px tall at 1440 px, arranged in two uneven rows. The upper row is approximately 422 and 649 px wide. The lower row is approximately 691 and 376 px wide. At 1024 px the large interface composition moves above the split row, with the documents card below. At 390 px the four cards are 320 px wide and 374, 219, 292 and 385 px tall.
- Recognition cards measure 416 × 362 px on wide screens with a 42 px gap, and 328 × 362 px with a 16 px gap on mobile. The wide About row is approximately 1059 × 442 px. The contact card measures about 502 × 617 px on wide screens. At 390 px the live reference shows the contact card but hides the FAQ controls, so the implementation includes a compact FAQ control on small screens that opens the full accordion above the contact card.
- At 1440 px the Work, editorial, recognition, About, FAQ and footer regions start near y = 899, 3591, 5502, 6284, 6809 and 7752. Full reference document height is 8105 px at the three widest sizes, 7844 px at 1024 px and 9606 px at the four mobile-variant sizes.

## Rendered type and color

- The display face is **Pockota Medium**, exposed as its own family at weight 400. The successful browser request was an OTF on the reference’s Framer asset CDN. The hero is 48/62.4 px on wide screens and 28/36.4 px in the inspected mobile DOM. Wide section headings are 48/57.6 px with −0.96 px tracking. Mobile section headings are typically 24/28.8 px, with a 28/33.6 px editorial-title variant. Wide project headings are 38/48.64 px and tablet/mobile headings are 24/30.72 px. The display face uses browser-default optical sizing in the reference.
- **Manrope** is the primary interface face. The availability label is 14/20 px at 600 and the CTA is 16/19.2 px at 700. Wide FAQ questions are 18/27 px at 600. The nav uses **Inter** at 14 px and weight 600. Inter also appears in detailed copy. Browser network logs also recorded Roboto and Inter Tight requests outside the homepage typography carried over here.
- The central colors are navy #081822, darker editorial surfaces #061219 and #07151d, white title text, subdued blue-gray supporting text, and mint accents. The four case surfaces are #2e4746, #1b2c2c, #172426 and #111c1d. The reference uses a tinted frosted navigation panel, understated borders, and a light inset CTA shadow. The implementation adjusts low-contrast supporting colors to pass automated contrast checks.
- Tokens and breakpoint values live in [src/tokens.css](../src/tokens.css). Manrope and Inter are self-hosted from Google Fonts with their SIL OFL files. The commercial Pockota webfont was absent from the workspace. The site uses Fraunces 600 under the SIL OFL for review. With an appropriate webfont license, set `POCKOTA_WEBFONT_PATH` to the licensed WOFF2 file at build time. The generator adds the self-hosted font and selects the reference weight. The Framer OTF was identified through network inspection and excluded from the project. [Pockota foundry and licensing](https://studionasir.com/fonts/pockota/)

## Interaction observations

- The desktop pill stays fixed and the work and FAQ items target the single page. The mobile navigation opens a rounded panel and collapses after selection. The rebuild uses the four destination names requested in the brief on both variants.
- The opening composition includes concentric arcs, a soft colored light, animated particles, a drifting employer strip and a footer band. Sections reveal during scrolling. Work art moves on hover. Services use image zoom, an angled interface composition, moving folded sheets and a moving point graphic. The recognition shelf moves horizontally.
- The FAQ starts with one open answer. Opening another closes the previous answer, grows the panel and rotates its icon. The implementation uses native named disclosures, animates supported browsers, and offers a reduced-motion state and motion pause control.
- Personal Folio is offered as a customizable portfolio template. The rebuild credits Sunal Sood and Shashi Kumar through their published template. Template-author photos, files, graphics and promotional links are absent from the site. [Personal Folio template announcement](https://dribbble.com/shots/22884196-Personal-folio-Portfolio-template)
