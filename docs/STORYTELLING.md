# Storytelling

The homepage opens with the portrait and role, followed by a slim employer strip. Three related enterprise stories share one frame. Atlas leads a short list of independent projects, followed by four career rows and a link to the personal story. Recognition and operating principles sit in separate disclosures. A workshop link and concise contact scene close the page.

Work is organized into Enterprise, Independent products, and Experiments and hackathons. Each case answers what was wrong, what Abhishek did and what changed. Illustrations, technical decisions, implementation evidence and historical notes expand on demand. Deep links reach the relevant entry directly.

About starts with the first family computer and the calculator that made software understandable. Design and Life Calculator explain the move into professional engineering. The page continues through enterprise growth, the exact IIT Guwahati qualification, influences, the workshop and a future hardware/software ambition.

The reference review covered the BusyBox loading treatment, hero, problem and solution sequences, process sections and closing at desktop and mobile sizes. The design borrows the use of illumination to show information moving, technical grids that give scenes structure, and a slower cadence around turning points. AXVRMA’s optical assembly, portrait integration, diagrams, type compositions and product treatments are original.
