# Storytelling

**The technology has changed. The curiosity hasn’t.**

The homepage leads with Abhishek Verma’s portrait, Senior Software Engineer role at DP World, 8+ years of experience and résumé access. The employer marquee reflects employment and internship history. Recognition and learning associations sit alongside the evidence in the milestones. “Impact beyond the code” introduces invoice automation and Nurtr product engineering before selected enterprise work.

## Page hierarchy

1. **Home** introduces the person, employer marquee, business impact and visual recognition, followed by selected enterprise work, pictured products, engineering principles, Story and Workshop links, and contact.
2. **Experience** carries the complete career chronology, employment history, six enterprise cases, awards and education. The invoice flagship shows the five-step extract-and-post workflow and a prominent outcome. Shipment workflows lead with the operational result and retain their interactive example. Once cleared, the same metrics appear under the corresponding employer and case.
3. **Work** retains seven flagship identities and 17 independent product case studies. The homepage leads with authentic Atlas and Envision screenshots. Vibes, Servio, Prism, Luminescent and Auditbench appear in a compact index. Their full pages carry motivations, architecture, demos and source links.
4. **Story** follows Curiosity, Understanding, Design meets engineering, Professional recognition, Enterprise engineering, Continuous learning and Beyond engineering. Native disclosures retain the early career detail and house the original desktop, arithmetic and Life Calculator experiences.
5. **Lab** pairs the owner’s laptop with a conceptual self-hosting illustration. It retains the homelab, LEGO, Raspberry Pi, Android ROMs, independent BoxBay AI simulation, hackathon builds and JARVIS-inspired aspiration.
6. **Engineering, About, Résumé and Contact** retain direct routes for philosophy, biography, printable credentials and ways to get in touch.

Home places company and team attribution next to any released metric. The default build uses concise qualitative outcomes while the exact figures await disclosure clearance. Technical detail, the complete timeline and fuller hackathon coverage remain at their existing destinations. Recruiters can reach the résumé from the first view. Hiring managers can move from the quality of the evidence into any professional case. Technical evaluators retain the existing architecture explanations and original demonstrations.

The design reference informed the placement of recognisable associations, portrait-led milestones and selected work. [Anirudh Goyal’s portfolio](https://anirudhgoyal.framer.website/)
