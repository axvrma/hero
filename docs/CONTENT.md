# Content editing

Edit the repository sources, then run `npm run verify` and review the rendered result. Keep pending evidence and restricted material outside the repository.

- **Profile, roles and credentials** live in `content/portfolio.json`. Keep the current job’s progression ordered. Home, About, the printable résumé and structured data derive from these records. Career chapters in `content/editorial.mjs` refer to employment by ID.
- **Enterprise work** is defined in `content/enterprise.mjs`. An entry renders at `/work#<id>`. Supply public context, the problem, personal and team contributions, constraints, decisions, trade-offs, outcome, lesson and a conceptual illustration. Leave proprietary implementation details in private documentation. Use fictional data for examples.
- **Flagship products** live in `content/products.mjs`. Keep slugs, supported destinations, original repository identities and evidence accurate. Editorial motivations are in `content/editorial.mjs`. Exhibition order and presentation live in the page components. Older identities remain in the registry’s aliases.
- **The archive and hackathons** use `content/legacy-projects.json`, optional new entries in `portfolio.json` and `content/hackathons.mjs`. The generator sanitizes the preserved HTML. `content/archive-overrides.json` records retired demos. The experiment record for BoxBay AI lives in `content/editorial.mjs`.
- **Metrics** stay pending until both evidence and disclosure clearance are recorded. Once cleared, add the approved value, unit, scope, baseline, outcome, organization, attribution and non-sensitive references for evidence and clearance to `content/publication.mjs`. The generator rejects incomplete approved metrics and displays cleared figures in the related homepage story and enterprise case. Approved source times are required for a derived reduction. [Publication review](CONTENT-REVIEW.md)
- **Images** are governed by the publication manifest and dedicated asset guide. [Asset integration](ASSETS.md)
